# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

**Expected behaviour:**
- Be respectful and constructive
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members

**Unacceptable behaviour:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to [privacy@apexmcp.ai](mailto:privacy@apexmcp.ai). All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers have the right to remove comments, issues, or users that violate this code.
