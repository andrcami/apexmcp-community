# Contributing to ApexMCP

Thanks for taking the time to help improve ApexMCP.

## How to contribute

### Report a bug
Open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/andrcami/apexmcp-community/issues/new?template=bug_report.md). Include steps to reproduce, your environment, and any error messages or screenshots.

### Request a feature
Open a [Feature Request](https://github.com/andrcami/apexmcp-community/issues/new?template=feature_request.md). Describe the problem you're solving, not just the solution you want.

### Ask a question or start a discussion
Use [Discussions](https://github.com/andrcami/apexmcp-community/discussions) — not Issues — for questions, ideas, and general chat.

## What happens after you submit

- We triage new issues within **3 business days**
- You'll receive an email notification when your issue is updated, commented on, or closed
- We tag issues with `planned`, `in progress`, `won't fix`, or `needs info` to keep you informed

## Issue hygiene

- Search before opening — your issue may already exist
- One issue per report — don't bundle multiple bugs into one ticket
- Include your ApexMCP version (shown in the sidebar footer)

## Code of Conduct

All participants are expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
